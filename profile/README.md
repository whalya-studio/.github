**Whalya is a venture studio.** It builds AI products that do real work for small teams: marketing, writing and the health of the business itself. Each product keeps a person in charge. Nothing goes out without your yes.

## Products

| Product | What it does | Links |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Whalyom** | Your entire marketing department inside Claude Code: 536 AI agents in 28 departments, 106 commands, 50 rules enforced in code. | [Repository](https://github.com/gilebehnam/whalyom) · [whalyom.com](https://whalyom.com) · open source |
| **Whalory** | Makes the AI you already use write like a professional copywriter, in English and Persian, with local linters. | [Repository](https://github.com/gilebehnam/whalory) · [whalory.com](https://whalory.com) · open-source core |
| **Whalyon** | A health check for your business: 40 statements, 8 areas, a report card that finds the root cause, not the symptom. | [Repository](https://github.com/gilebehnam/whalyon) · [whalyon.com](https://whalyon.com) · live, free launch |

## How we build

- **Problem first.** A product starts from a problem a small team actually has, not from a model.
- **Facts you can check.** Numbers on our pages are counted from the files or marked as company-reported.
- **English and Persian.** Every product is built to work well in both, right to left included.
